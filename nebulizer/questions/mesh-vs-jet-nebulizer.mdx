## How Each Type Works

**Jet nebulizer:** an electric compressor pushes air through liquid medicine to make a mist. The compressor is noisy and bulky and usually needs a wall outlet.

**Vibrating mesh nebulizer:** a mesh with thousands of tiny holes vibrates and pushes liquid through, making a fine mist. There's no compressor, so it's quiet.

## Why Particle Size Matters

Particles of about 1 to 5 microns are small enough to reach the smaller airways. Larger particles tend to land in the mouth and throat. The MistPro manual says at least 60% of its mist, by volume, is in particles under 5 microns, with a median of 1 to 4 microns (±25%).

## When a Jet Nebulizer Might Be Preferred

- Your medicine is a suspension (such as budesonide), which the MistPro manual says not to use
- Heavy clinical use
- When an outlet is always available and portability doesn't matter
