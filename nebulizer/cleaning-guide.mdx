Proper cleaning of the MistPro is essential for hygiene, preventing infection, and maintaining device performance. The mesh component is the critical element — a clogged or contaminated mesh reduces nebulization efficiency.

## After Every Use: Clean and Disinfect

The MistPro manual says to clean and disinfect the device after each use, and to dry it right after cleaning. If it isn't cleaned, dried medicine can build up on the mesh and weaken the mist. Do not hold the device under tap water, because water can get inside it.

### Step 1: Take It Apart

- Remove the medication cup and the mask from the device body
- Open the cup lid and pour out any leftover medicine

### Step 2: Disinfect the Medication Cup

Add 75% ethanol (alcohol) solution to the medication cup, close the lid, and leave it for at least 10 minutes. You can shake it gently.

### Step 3: Rinse Well

Pour out the ethanol. Rinse the medication cup with clean water several times. Rinse off all of the disinfectant: the manual warns that leftover disinfectant can make symptoms worse.

### Step 4: Run Clean Water Through the Mesh

Fill the medication cup with clean water, attach it to the device body, and run the device for 10 minutes to clean the mesh. Don't breathe in the mist while it runs: the manual says not to inhale water.

### Step 5: Dry Right Away

Wipe away the water with new medical gauze and let all parts dry fully.

### Step 6: Wipe the Device Body

Wipe the outside of the device body with 75% medical alcohol, then let it air-dry or wipe it dry with a clean, soft cloth. Do not rinse the main device body (containing the electronic components and battery) with water or put it in water.

### Step 7: Store

Store the device body, medication cup, and masks in a clean, dry place.

### Masks

Each user must use their own mask and accessories, as the manual says. The manual calls the accessories single-use. Contact SonoHealth support with any questions about the masks.

**Do not** put the medication cup or masks in boiling water, because the parts can lose their shape. **Do not** dry them in a microwave.

## Mesh Care: The Most Important Component

The vibrating mesh is the precision component of the MistPro. Proper care extends its life and maintains treatment efficiency:

**Do:**
- Clean and disinfect the medication cup (which houses the mesh) after every use, as described above
- Use distilled water rather than tap water if your area has hard water (prevents mineral deposits)
- Store with the medication cup empty and dry

**Do not:**
- Insert any object into the mesh holes (pins, cotton swabs, brushes)
- Use harsh chemical cleaners such as bleach or acetone
- Allow the cup to sit with residual medication in it for extended periods
- Subject the mesh side to hard water without cleaning — mineral buildup reduces mesh efficiency

### If the Mist Gets Weak

The MistPro manual says weak or no mist can mean the mesh is dirty. Follow every step above, including the 10-minute clean-water run (Step 4) and drying right away. The mesh is a part that wears out: the manual gives its life as about six months when used 3 times a day for 20 minutes each time. Mesh life varies with use, medicine, and cleaning. If there is still little or no mist after cleaning, replace the medication cup or contact SonoHealth support.

## Storage

- Store the MistPro in a **clean, dry location** — a case or sealed bag prevents dust accumulation on the mesh
- Do not store with any liquid in the medication cup
- Store at **room temperature** — avoid extreme heat, cold, or humidity
- Keep the device away from direct sunlight for extended periods

## When to Replace Components

| Component | Replace When |
|---|---|
| Face mask | The manual calls the accessories single-use; contact SonoHealth support with questions about masks |
| Medication cup | Discoloration, cracks, or persistent mesh clogging that doesn't resolve |
| Device body | Contact SonoHealth support if cleaning and replacing the medication cup do not restore mist output |

The MistPro's 2-year warranty covers device defects. If the device fails to nebulize adequately after following cleaning procedures, contact SonoHealth support.
