## Clean and disinfect after every use

The MistPro SN-07 manual says to "Clean and disinfect the device after each use. If the device doesn't clean up, the nebulizing will be affected because of drying and coagulation of medication." A weekly routine is not enough.

## Step by step (from the manual)

1. Remove the medicine cup and accessories from the device body.
2. Open the cup lid and pour out any leftover medicine.
3. Add 75% ethanol (alcohol) solution to the medicine cup, close the lid, and leave it for at least 10 minutes. You can shake it gently.
4. Soak the accessories in a covered container of 75% ethanol solution for 10 minutes or longer.
5. Pour out the ethanol. Rinse the medicine cup and accessories with clean water several times.
6. Fill the medicine cup with clean water, attach it to the device body, and let the device run for 10 minutes. This cleans the mesh.
7. Wipe away the water with new medical gauze and let everything dry fully.
8. Wipe the outside of the device body with 75% medicinal alcohol. Then let it air-dry or wipe it dry with a clean, soft cloth.
9. Store the device body, medicine cup and accessories in a clean, dry place.

**Rinse away all disinfectant before the next treatment.** The manual warns that leftover disinfectant "may cause symptomatic deterioration." The water run in step 6 is a cleaning step only. Don't breathe it in as a treatment.

## Protect the mesh and the device

- **Don't touch the metal mesh** with a cotton swab or anything sharp. The manual says the device "may not work" afterward.
- **Don't boil or microwave** the medicine cup or accessories.
- **Don't rinse or soak the main unit** in water, and don't hold it under a tap while cleaning the cup.
- **Don't use bleach, acetone or other harsh chemical cleaners.** Use only the 75% ethanol steps above.
- **Store it in a clean, dry place at room temperature.** Avoid humidity, high heat, extreme cold and long periods of direct sunlight. Don't store it with liquid in the medicine cup.

## Accessories: one person each

The manual says each user must use their own accessories, and that some accessories are designed for single use. If you're not sure which parts in your box can be reused, ask [SonoHealth support](https://support.sonohealth.com). Don't share a mask between people.

## When to replace parts

The manual says the mesh ("nebulizing sheet") wears out. At 3 treatments a day of 20 minutes each, it lasts about 6 months, and less with heavier use. If little or no mist comes out after a full clean, a new medicine cup may be needed.

Replace a part sooner, whatever its age, if:

- The **mask** is cracked, worn or deteriorating, or you have hygiene concerns after an illness.
- The **medicine cup** is cracked or discolored, or it stays clogged after a full clean.

Don't keep using a damaged part. Contact SonoHealth support for replacements.
