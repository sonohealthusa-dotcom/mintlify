The SonoHealth MistPro (model SN-07) is a portable vibrating mesh nebulizer that turns liquid medicine into a fine mist you breathe in. It's quiet (50 dB or less), runs on a rechargeable battery, and comes with adult and child masks. Answers below come from the SN-07 user manual unless noted.

## Product & Technology

**What is the MistPro Nebulizer?** A handheld mesh nebulizer from SonoHealth. A vibrating mesh turns liquid medicine into a fine mist for inhalation. It comes with masks for adults and children. See the current price on the [product page](https://sonohealth.com/products/portable-mesh-nebulizer-respiratory-relief/).

**What technology does MistPro use?**
Vibrating mesh (piezoelectric). The manual says at least 60% of the mist particles are smaller than 5 microns. There's no compressor, so it's quiet.

**Do I need a doctor's guidance?** Yes. The manual says the product "should be purchased and used under the guidance of a doctor," and to follow your doctor's advice on the medicine, dose and use.

**What is included with the MistPro?**
The device body, medicine cup, adult mask, child mask, power cord and user manual. A power adapter is optional. Medicine is not included.

## Setup and Use

**How do I use the MistPro?** Add your prescribed medicine (2 ml to 10 ml) to the cup, close the lid, attach the cup until it clicks, and fit the mask. Press ON/OFF. About 1 second later the light turns green and misting starts. Hold the device steady and breathe the mist in slowly. See [How It Works](/nebulizer/how-it-works).

**Does the nebulizer come with medication?**
No. SonoHealth doesn't sell medicine. Most nebulizer medicines need a prescription from your doctor. Sterile saline for inhalation is sold without one, for example on Amazon.

**Can I use saline in it?**
Ask your clinician whether saline is right for you, and how much to use.

**Can this be used on a child or infant?** The manual says a guardian must be present when a child uses the device. SonoHealth's support FAQ says it can be used with an infant when using saline for inhalation. Always ask your child's pediatrician first, and follow their instructions on medicine and amount.

**How long does a treatment take?**
It depends on how much medicine is in the cup and the mist setting. The manual lists mist rates of at least 0.25 ml/min (setting I) and at least 0.15 ml/min (setting II). Keep each session to 20 minutes or less, and stop and talk to a doctor if you feel any discomfort.

## Medications

**What medications can I use with MistPro?** The manual names solutions such as terbutaline sulphate and ipratropium bromide as commonly used. It says not to use suspensions, so budesonide (Pulmicort Respules) is not for the MistPro. Always follow your doctor's advice. See [Which medicines work](/nebulizer/medications-compatible).

**Can I mix medications in the cup?**
Only if your doctor or pharmacist tells you to. Don't mix or dilute medicines on your own.

**What should I NOT put in the nebulizer?** Suspensions; high-concentration, high-viscosity, oily or volatile medicines; water (the manual says "Do not use the device to inhale water"); essential oils; or anything not labeled for inhalation.

## Cleaning and Maintenance

**How do I clean the MistPro?** The manual says to clean and disinfect after every use: soak the medicine cup and accessories in 75% ethanol for at least 10 minutes, rinse well with clean water, run the device with clean water for 10 minutes, then dry. Never touch the mesh or rinse the main unit. See the [Cleaning Guide](/nebulizer/cleaning-guide) for every step.

**Can I boil or microwave the parts?** No. The manual says not to put the medicine cup or accessories in boiling water or a microwave.

**The mist is weak. How do I fix it?** Clean the medicine cup as the manual describes, and never poke the mesh with a cotton swab or anything sharp. If the mist is still weak, the mesh may be worn out: the manual says it lasts about 6 months at 3 sessions a day. See [Troubleshooting](/nebulizer/troubleshooting).

## Battery and Charging

**How long does the battery last?** About 1 hour of use on a full charge.

**How do I charge it?** Plug the power cord's DC end into the device and the other end into a power source. The light glows blue while charging and turns off when it's done. The manual specifies a 5V, 1.0A adapter.

**Can I replace the battery?** No. The manual says the battery can't be replaced by the user. If you store the device, charge it about every six months.
