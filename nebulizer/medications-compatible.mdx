## Start here: check your medicine against this device

The MistPro SN-07 manual says: "Do not use suspended or high-concentration medicinal solutions." It also says not to use medicine that is "high viscosity, oily," suspended or volatile.

That means **budesonide inhalation suspension (Pulmicort Respules) should not be used in the MistPro.** If your prescription is a suspension, ask your prescriber or pharmacist which nebulizer fits it. Don't change your medicine or dose on your own.

## Medicines the manual names

The manual says commonly used nebulized medicines include moistening expectorants, bronchodilators and antibiotics, "such as terbutaline sulphate solution, ipratropium bromide solution."

It also says: "Please follow doctors' advice about medication species, dosage and usage." So for any prescribed medicine:

1. Ask your pharmacist: "Is this a solution or a suspension?" Solutions can be considered for this device. Suspensions can't.
2. Use only the amount and schedule your prescriber gives you.
3. Before first use, ask your doctor to confirm the medicine is right for a mesh nebulizer. The manual says: "Before using any pharmaceutical products or medicines, please consult your doctor to ensure that you are using the product correctly."

## The MistPro doesn't include medicine

The MistPro turns liquid medicine into a fine mist you breathe in. Most breathing medicines need a prescription. Sterile saline for inhalation is sold without one. Ask your clinician whether saline is right for you, and how much to use, especially for a child.

## What not to put in the cup

- Suspensions, such as budesonide (Pulmicort Respules)
- High-concentration, thick (high-viscosity), oily or volatile liquids
- Water. The manual says: "Do not use the device to inhale water."
- Essential oils, oral medicines, or anything not labeled for inhalation
- Don't dilute or mix medicines to make them fit the device

## Children

The manual says a guardian must be present when a child uses the device. Ask the child's clinician to confirm the medicine, the amount and how long each session should last.

## Quick facts from the manual

- The medicine cup holds 2 ml to 10 ml.
- Each session should be no longer than 20 minutes. If you feel any discomfort, stop and talk to a doctor.
- Use fresh medicine every time. The manual says used medicine "can't be reused."
