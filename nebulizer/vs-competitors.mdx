## MistPro's Key Advantages

### 1. Adult and Child Masks Included

The MistPro includes **both adult and child masks** in the box, so one device can serve the family (each person should use their own accessories, and a guardian must be present when a child uses it, per the manual).

### 2. Quiet Mesh Design

The manual lists noise at 50 dB or less. With no compressor, it's much quieter than a jet nebulizer, which helps with children and night-time treatments.

### 3. Physician Training Videos

SonoHealth includes a **free video training series by Dr. Carolina Melgar, MD** for MistPro users.

### 4. 60-Day Returns

The MistPro comes with a **60-day return policy with a full refund** from SonoHealth.

### 5. 2-Year Warranty

The MistPro is covered by a 2-year warranty when bought from SonoHealth.

## When Competitors May Be Preferred

**PARI Trek S:** A jet nebulizer (not mesh) with a strong clinical reputation. Some pulmonologists prefer PARI systems for specific medicines, such as in cystic fibrosis care. For medicines that are suspensions (which the MistPro manual says not to use), ask your doctor whether a jet nebulizer is the better fit.

**Omron MicroAir:** A well-regarded mesh nebulizer with strong retail distribution. See the full head-to-head: [MistPro vs Omron MicroAir](/nebulizer/mistpro-vs-omron-microair).

**Unbranded marketplace options:** Check the label and the FDA database before using one with prescribed medicine.
