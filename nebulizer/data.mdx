## Regulatory & Safety

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Device Class | Nebulizers are Class II medical devices in the US |
| Use Under a Doctor | The manual says the product "should be purchased and used under the guidance of a doctor" |
| Intended Use | Inhaling medicine that the device turns into a mist |
| Children | A guardian must be present when a child uses the device |
| Session Length | 20 minutes or less per session |
| Medication Compatibility | Prescribed solutions such as terbutaline sulphate or ipratropium bromide solution (named in the manual). Not for suspensions (e.g., budesonide), or high-concentration, high-viscosity, oily or volatile liquids |
| Not For | Inhaling water; oil-based substances or essential oils |
| Cleaning | Clean and disinfect after every use with 75% ethanol (see the [Cleaning Guide](/nebulizer/cleaning-guide)) |

## Conditions

The manual says the device can deliver medicine for respiratory conditions such as acute upper respiratory inflammation, acute and chronic tracheitis, and bronchitis. Your doctor decides the medicine and treatment for your condition.

## Safety Statement

Use the MistPro only with medicine your doctor prescribes or approves. Do not use suspensions or oil-based substances. Clean and disinfect after every use, and rinse away all disinfectant before the next treatment. Contact your doctor if symptoms get worse or don't improve.
