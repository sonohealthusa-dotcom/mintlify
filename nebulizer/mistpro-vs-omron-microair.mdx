Both the SonoHealth MistPro and the Omron MicroAir are quiet, hand-sized vibrating mesh nebulizers. The MistPro ships with both adult and child masks and comes with a 2-year warranty and 60-day returns when bought from SonoHealth. Omron is a long-established brand with its own accessories. Check Omron's current specs, warranty and FDA listing for the model you're considering.

<Note>
A nebulizer is a drug-delivery device. It does not include medicine, and most breathing medicines need a prescription. Use only medicines your doctor prescribes or approves, and follow their instructions.
</Note>

Both use the same basic vibrating-mesh principle, so day-to-day use is similar: quiet, portable and efficient. The differences that matter most for buyers are what comes in the box, the warranty and returns, and whether the device fits the whole family.

## Which one is better for treating children?

The MistPro includes both an adult mask and a child mask in the box, so one device can serve a child and an adult (each person should use their own accessories, per the manual). Check which masks come with the Omron package you're considering. For quiet treatments with a sleeping child, a mesh unit is much quieter than a jet nebulizer. See [nebulizer for kids](/nebulizer/nebulizer-for-kids-guide).

## Is the warranty and return policy different?

The MistPro comes with a 2-year warranty and a 60-day money-back guarantee when bought from SonoHealth. For the MicroAir, warranty and return terms depend on Omron and the retailer, so check them before you buy. Buying the MistPro direct from [SonoHealth](/nebulizer/buy) gets you an authentic device and full support.

## Do both work with the same medications?

The MistPro manual names prescribed solutions such as terbutaline sulphate and ipratropium bromide, and says not to use suspensions (such as budesonide). Check the Omron manual for its own list. Neither should be used with oil-based substances or essential oils. See [Which medicines work](/nebulizer/medications-compatible), and confirm your medicine with your pharmacist.

## Which should you choose?

Choose the MistPro if you want adult and child masks included, a 2-year warranty and a 60-day return window. Choose the Omron MicroAir if you prefer that brand or already own its accessories. Either way, a mesh nebulizer is quieter and more portable than a jet unit. See [mesh vs jet](/nebulizer/mesh-vs-jet).

<Warning>
A nebulizer cannot replace emergency care. If breathing is severe, a rescue inhaler is not helping, or lips or face look blue, call emergency services. See [when to seek urgent care](/nebulizer/asthma-relief).
</Warning>
