## What Is a Mesh Nebulizer?

A mesh nebulizer uses a thin metal mesh with many tiny holes. A piezoelectric plate vibrates the mesh, pushing liquid medicine through the holes as a fine mist. The manual says at least 60% of the particles are smaller than 5 microns, with a median size of about 1 to 4 microns.

Compared with older jet nebulizers, which use an air compressor, a mesh nebulizer is:

- Quieter, with no compressor
- Small and battery-powered, so it's easy to take with you
- Simple to run with one button

## Key Features

**Portable and compact:** fits in your hand, for use at home or while traveling.

**One-button operation:** press ON/OFF. About 1 second later the light turns green and misting starts. Press again to switch between the two mist settings.

**Rechargeable battery:** about 1 hour of use per full charge.

**Masks for adults and children:** both are in the box. The manual says a guardian must be present when a child uses the device, and each person should use their own accessories.

## Before You Use It

- Check your medicine: the manual says not to use suspended, high-concentration, high-viscosity, oily or volatile medicines. See [Which medicines work](/nebulizer/medications-compatible).
- Clean and disinfect after every use. See the [Cleaning Guide](/nebulizer/cleaning-guide).
- Keep each session to 20 minutes or less. If you feel any discomfort, stop and talk to a doctor.
