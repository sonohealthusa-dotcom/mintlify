These fixes come from the MistPro SN-07 manual (Chapter 6, Troubleshooting). Always turn the device off before you check or clean anything.

## No mist, or very little mist

| Likely cause | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Medicine cup not fully installed | Remove the cup and put it back until you hear a click. |
| No medicine in the cup | Add your prescribed medicine (2 ml to 10 ml). |
| The medicine isn't suitable for this device | Talk to your doctor. Suspensions and thick, oily or high-concentration medicines should not be used. See [Which medicines work](/nebulizer/medications-compatible). |
| The mesh is dirty | Clean the medicine cup as described in the [Cleaning Guide](/nebulizer/cleaning-guide). |
| Low battery | Charge the device. |

Don't touch the metal mesh with a cotton swab, brush or anything sharp. The manual says that can stop the device from working. Never use bleach or other harsh chemical cleaners on the cup or mesh; clean only with the 75% ethanol steps in the [Cleaning Guide](/nebulizer/cleaning-guide). If mist output stays low after a full clean, the mesh may be worn out. The manual says it lasts about 6 months at 3 sessions a day of 20 minutes each. Contact support about a new medicine cup.

## Device won't start

- **Low or dead battery:** charge it. If the light comes on for about a second and then goes out, the battery is likely empty.
- **Light comes on, then goes out:** the medicine cup may not be installed, or the cup may be empty. Reinstall the cup and add medicine.
- **How to turn it on:** press the ON/OFF button. About 1 second later the light turns green and misting starts. The device is designed to need a press of about 1 second, so it doesn't turn on by accident in a bag.

## Device doesn't shut off when the medicine runs out

The manual lists three causes. For each, turn the device off first:

- **Bubbles in the cup:** clear the bubbles.
- **Medicine stuck on the mesh:** clean the medicine cup.
- **Dirty electrodes where the cup meets the body:** clean the electrodes.

If it still doesn't shut off on its own, press ON/OFF to stop it. That protects the mesh.

## Battery runs out quickly

- It may not be fully charged. The light glows blue while charging and turns off when charging is done. A full charge runs about 1 hour.
- If it still runs short after a full charge, the battery may be damaged. The battery can't be replaced by the user. Contact SonoHealth support.
- If you store the device for a long time, charge it about every six months.

## Water around the mouthpiece

The manual says this is condensation, caused by the temperature difference between the medicine and the air. Remove the medicine cup and pour the water out.

## Tips for a smooth treatment

- Hold the device steady and upright. Don't shake it.
- Don't cover the small air hole on the cup lid.
- When the medicine is nearly gone, tilt the device slightly toward you so the rest reaches the mesh.
- Keep each session to 20 minutes or less. If you feel discomfort, stop and talk to a doctor.
- A little medicine left in the cup after auto shut-off is normal.

## When to contact SonoHealth support

- Mist doesn't come back after a full clean
- The device doesn't charge, or the battery runs short after a full charge
- The device looks damaged
