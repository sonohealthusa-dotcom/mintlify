## Why men often need more magnesium

Magnesium is involved in over 300 enzymatic reactions, including muscle contraction, energy (ATP) production, and the stress response. Several factors common in men can lower magnesium status:

- **Heavy training and sweating** deplete magnesium
- **Higher muscle mass** raises baseline demand
- **Processed diets, alcohol, and caffeine** reduce intake or increase losses
- **Chronic stress** drives magnesium excretion

The RDA for adult men is **400–420&nbsp;mg per day**, and many men fall short from food alone.
