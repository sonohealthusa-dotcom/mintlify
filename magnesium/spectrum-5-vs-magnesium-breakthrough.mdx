## What actually matters when comparing

### 1. Elemental magnesium per serving

The number that determines how much magnesium you actually get is the elemental (total) magnesium per serving, not the count of forms. Spectrum 5 states **500&nbsp;mg per two-capsule serving**, a clinically relevant amount for adults supplementing to close a dietary gap. When you evaluate any competitor, find the "magnesium" line on the Supplement Facts panel and compare like-for-like.

### 2. Number of forms vs absorption

Additional forms only help if they meaningfully raise absorbed magnesium or add a specific, wanted target. Spectrum 5's five forms already span the major pathways — glycinate for calm and sleep, malate for energy and muscle, citrate for metabolism, and oxide/hydroxide for regularity. Breakthrough adds forms such as magnesium L-threonate (studied for cognition) and taurate; if those specific targets matter to you, that's a genuine point in its favor.

### 3. Sourcing and trace minerals

Spectrum 5 is marine-sourced via Aquamin, from *Lithothamnion* red algae, which supplies 72+ naturally occurring trace minerals alongside magnesium. Magnesium works with cofactors like zinc, boron, and selenium, so this mineral context is a differentiator that a purely synthetic multi-form blend doesn't provide.

### 4. Price and value

Spectrum 5 is positioned as a high-value option. Breakthrough typically sits at a higher price point. If you're cost-conscious and want a high verified dose, Spectrum 5 usually wins on dose-per-dollar.

## Which should you choose?

**Choose Spectrum 5 if you want:** a high 500&nbsp;mg elemental dose, marine trace minerals, batch-level third-party testing, US manufacturing, and strong value with a 60-day money-back guarantee.

**Consider Magnesium Breakthrough if you specifically want:** magnesium L-threonate for cognitive goals or taurate, and you're comfortable paying more for a longer list of forms.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a seven-form magnesium better than a five-form?**
Not inherently. What matters is total absorbed magnesium, form quality, dose, and testing. Five well-chosen forms at 500&nbsp;mg can outperform seven forms at a lower dose.

**Does Spectrum 5 contain magnesium L-threonate or taurate?**
No. Spectrum 5 uses glycinate, malate, citrate, oxide, and hydroxide. If a clinician has specifically recommended threonate or taurate, factor that into your choice.

**Which is better value?**
Spectrum 5 generally offers more elemental magnesium per dollar, with a subscription option.
