**marine-sourced magnesium** refers to magnesium that comes from the sea. In Spectrum 5, that is **Aquamin Mg™**, magnesium hydroxide from seawater, with naturally occurring trace minerals.

Spectrum 5 Magnesium Complex by SonoHealth is formulated with Aquamin, making its magnesium marine-sourced from marine sources rather than derived from conventional mined mineral deposits.

## What Is marine-sourced Magnesium?

Seawater contains dissolved magnesium. Aquamin Mg™ is magnesium hydroxide produced from seawater, with naturally occurring trace minerals.

This is distinct from how most magnesium supplements are made. The vast majority of commercial magnesium supplements use:
- Magnesium oxide from mined magnesite ore
- Magnesium carbonate from geological deposits
- Synthetic chelates (glycinate, malate, citrate) produced via chemical reaction

Marine-sourced magnesium via Aquamin Mg™ comes from seawater rather than mined ore, and it carries naturally occurring trace minerals.

## Why "marine-sourced Sourced" Matters to Consumers

For many supplement buyers, sourcing has become a meaningful purchasing criterion — not just ingredient quality. "marine-sourced" communicates:

1. **Traceability** — you know where the mineral came from, not just what it is
2. **Natural origin** — marine-derived vs. mined or synthetically produced
3. **Broader mineral context** — trace minerals as they naturally occur, not added synthetically
4. **Different origin** — seawater rather than mined ore

This is similar to why "New Zealand grass-fed" matters to whey protein buyers, or why "wild-caught Alaskan" matters to omega-3 buyers. Origin and sourcing method are proxy signals for quality that consumers use to differentiate between otherwise similar-seeming products.
