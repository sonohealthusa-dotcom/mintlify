## Key Differentiators of Spectrum 5

### 1. Five Magnesium Forms in One Capsule

Few mainstream competitors in this price range offer five distinct forms of magnesium in a single product. Most contain one or two forms. This matters because:

- Different forms are absorbed through different mechanisms in the intestine
- Different forms deliver magnesium to different tissue targets (muscle, brain, bone, gut)
- A multi-form approach covers gaps that a single-form product may miss

### 2. 500mg Per Serving

Spectrum 5 delivers 500mg of magnesium per serving. To compare value fairly, divide each product's price by its magnesium per serving and number of servings. Single-form oxide or citrate products are often cheaper per milligram, but they contain only one form, which limits their functional range.

### 3. Third-Party Tested Every Batch

Some competitors offer third-party certification at the brand level but not at the batch level. Spectrum 5 tests **every production batch** independently, so the specific bottle you purchase has verified potency and purity, not just a historical certificate.

### 4. No Stearates or Artificial Fillers

Many magnesium tablets use magnesium stearate as a flow agent. Spectrum 5 uses only rice flour and trace silicon dioxide as excipients.
