## How to Compare Magnesium Products at the Same Price

When evaluating competing magnesium complexes in this range, ask:

1. **How many forms?** More forms means broader coverage.
2. **What's the dose?** Check the magnesium line on the Supplement Facts panel.
3. **Is there a trace mineral story?** Most products have none. Aquamin is a real differentiator.
4. **Is the source named?** "Aquamin" > anonymous "marine magnesium" > unnamed commodity oxide.
5. **Is it third-party tested?** This verifies the label is accurate.
6. **What's in the capsule besides magnesium?** Stearates, titanium dioxide, and artificial dyes add nothing.
