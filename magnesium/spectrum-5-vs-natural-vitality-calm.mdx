<Note>
**Quick answer:** Calm is a citrate-only powder — simple and pleasant, but it targets a narrower range. Spectrum 5 is a multi-form capsule that also includes glycinate (calm/sleep), malate (energy/muscle), and marine trace minerals, covering more of magnesium's roles from one product.
</Note>

## When each one shines

### Natural Vitality Calm is a good fit if you:
- Prefer a warm or cold **drink** over swallowing capsules
- Mainly want **relaxation and bowel regularity** (citrate has a mild laxative effect)
- Like to **titrate the dose** by adjusting the scoop

### Spectrum 5 is a good fit if you:
- Want **one product** to cover sleep, muscle recovery, energy, stress, and digestion
- Prefer the convenience of **capsules** with no mixing or taste
- Value **glycinate** for calm/sleep and **malate** for energy — forms Calm doesn't include
- Want **marine trace minerals** and batch-level third-party testing

## Why form breadth matters

Magnesium citrate alone is well studied and effective for metabolism, mood, and regularity, but it doesn't cover the glycine-GABA calming pathway (glycinate) or ATP energy production and muscle recovery (malate) as directly. Spectrum 5 combines citrate with four other forms so a single daily serving works across multiple pathways at once, plus the cofactor trace minerals from Aquamin.

<Warning>
Magnesium citrate's laxative effect can be pronounced at higher doses. Whichever product you choose, take magnesium with food, start at a modest dose, and consult your doctor if you have kidney disease, are pregnant or nursing, or take medications. ## Frequently asked questions

**Is a magnesium powder better absorbed than capsules?**
Not necessarily. Dissolved citrate is well absorbed, but well-formulated capsules like Spectrum 5's — dominated by glycinate and other bioavailable forms — are also well absorbed. Format is mostly about preference.

**Can I use both?**
It's possible, but count magnesium from all sources toward your daily total to avoid loose stools or excess intake. Space doses and check with your doctor if unsure.

**Which is better for sleep?**
For sleep specifically, glycinate (in Spectrum 5) is often preferred over citrate alone. Calm can still aid relaxation, but Spectrum 5 targets sleep more directly.
