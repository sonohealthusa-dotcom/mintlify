The best air purifier for a night-shift worker or daytime sleeper is one that runs **quiet enough to sleep beside at any hour, dims its display, and keeps cleaning the air around the clock**. The SonoHealth AirPro's **25 dB Night Mode** is quieter than a whisper and dims its lights, while medical-grade **HEPA 14** filtration (99.9% of particles down to 0.1 microns) keeps your room clean whether you sleep at 2 a.m. or 2 p.m. It is a wellness appliance, not a medical device.

## Why daytime sleep is harder on air quality

Sleeping during the day means resting when the household — and the outdoor environment — is at its busiest: more foot traffic stirs up dust, cooking and cleaning add particles and odors, and daytime is often when outdoor pollen and traffic pollution peak. A purifier that runs continuously and quietly helps keep your sleep space clean even while the rest of the world is active. See [Do air purifiers work?](/air-purifier/do-air-purifiers-work)

## How quiet is 25 dB, really?

At **25 dB**, the AirPro's Night Mode is quieter than a soft whisper or a library — well below a normal conversation (about 60 dB). For many daytime sleepers the gentle, steady airflow doubles as light white noise that masks daytime household sounds. On higher fan speeds it reaches up to 50 dB when you want a faster clean before bed. See [Smart Features](/air-purifier/smart-features).

## Run it continuously for round-the-clock clean air

Because the AirPro is **ozone-free** (no ionizer) and its **UV-C is fully enclosed**, it is safe to leave running 24/7. Set it on Night Mode or a low speed before you sleep, and let it keep working while you rest. The built-in tracker tells you when the [HEPA 14 filter](/air-purifier/hepa-14-filtration) needs replacing (every 6–12 months), and [running costs](/air-purifier/air-purifier-running-costs) stay low for continuous use.

<Warning>
An air purifier is not a treatment for insomnia, sleep apnea, shift-work sleep disorder, or any medical condition. It reduces airborne particles but does not diagnose or treat disease. If disrupted sleep affects your health, talk to a clinician.
</Warning>

## Where should I place it?

Put the AirPro a few feet from the bed with a few inches of clearance on all sides so its 360-degree intake can draw air freely — not tucked in a corner or under furniture. See [Where to place an air purifier](/air-purifier/where-to-place-an-air-purifier).
