Rabbit Air's MinusA2 and A3 are premium, design-forward, wall-mountable purifiers with customizable filters and app control — but they cost several times more than the SonoHealth AirPro and use a **True HEPA (BioGS, ~99.97% at 0.3 microns)** filter. The AirPro uses a higher **medical-grade HEPA 14 (99.995% at 0.3 microns, EN 1822)** plus fully enclosed, ozone-free **UV-C**, at a far lower price. Rabbit Air wins on aesthetics, customization, and smart features; the AirPro wins on filtration grade and value.

## Which filters more thoroughly?

Both are strong mechanical purifiers, but they sit a grade apart. Rabbit Air's **BioGS HEPA** is a **True HEPA-class** filter rated around **99.97% at 0.3 microns**. The AirPro's **HEPA 14** is certified to **99.995% at 0.3 microns** under EN 1822 — roughly **10× fewer particles** slip through at the most-penetrating size, and it captures 99.9% down to 0.1 microns. See [HEPA 14 vs. True HEPA](/air-purifier/hepa-14-vs-true-hepa).

Where Rabbit Air answers back is **customization**: you can choose a filter tuned for germs, odors, toxins, or pet allergies. The AirPro takes a fixed 3-stage approach (pre-filter, HEPA 14, carbon) and adds UV-C on top.

## Does UV-C matter here?

The AirPro adds a **fully enclosed UV-C (254 nm)** stage that targets bacteria and mold captured on the filter — useful in [damp or mold-prone rooms](/air-purifier/best-air-purifier-for-mold). Rabbit Air relies on its filter plus an **optional negative-ion** feature rather than UV-C; the ion feature can be switched off, and Rabbit Air markets its units as ozone-safe. The AirPro has **no ionizer at all** and is ozone-free by design — see [HEPA vs. ionizer purifiers](/air-purifier/hepa-vs-ionizer-air-purifiers) and [UV-C sterilization](/air-purifier/uv-c-sterilization).

## Design, smart features, and coverage

This is Rabbit Air's home turf. The MinusA2 and A3 **mount on a wall**, come with decorative front panels, and include a **Wi-Fi app, air-quality sensors, and auto mode** — a genuinely premium, hands-off experience. The AirPro is a freestanding unit with 360-degree intake, a 2/4/6-hour Smart Timer, a 25 dB Night Mode, and a filter tracker, with **no app required**.

On coverage, both brands publish area figures at different air-change rates, so match each to your room using [room coverage](/air-purifier/room-coverage) and [CADR vs. ACH explained](/air-purifier/cadr-vs-ach-explained) rather than comparing headline numbers.

## Which should you buy?

- **Choose Rabbit Air** if wall-mounting, decorative design, filter customization, and app automation justify a premium price, and True HEPA is enough for you.
- **Choose the AirPro** if you want the higher **HEPA 14** grade plus **UV-C**, quiet 25 dB night operation, and a 60-day risk-free trial at roughly a quarter of the price.

<Note>
No air purifier treats or prevents illness. Both reduce airborne particles and, where equipped, microorganisms in the air that passes through them; they are not a substitute for medical care or ventilation. Consult a clinician for health concerns.
</Note>

## Bottom line

Rabbit Air is the premium design-and-customization choice; the AirPro is the higher-grade, UV-C-equipped value choice with a **60-day trial**. For buyers who care most about filtration grade and price, the AirPro delivers cleanroom-class HEPA 14 for a fraction of the cost.
