The best air purifier for mold is a well-sealed unit with a high-grade HEPA filter (HEPA 13–14) sized to your room, ideally paired with activated carbon for musty odors and UV-C to stop captured spores from regrowing on the filter. The SonoHealth AirPro combines medical-grade HEPA 14, a 3-stage filter, and fully enclosed UV-C across up to 1,200 sq ft. Importantly, a purifier reduces airborne spores — it does not remove mold from surfaces or replace fixing the moisture that feeds it.

<Warning>
An air purifier is not a treatment for mold illness and does not remediate mold growing on walls, grout, or ceilings. If you have health symptoms you attribute to mold, see a clinician, and fix the moisture source and remediate visible mold.
</Warning>

## Can an air purifier actually help with mold?

Yes — for the airborne part. Mold spores are roughly 1–30 microns, so a HEPA 14 filter that captures 99.9% of particles down to 0.1 microns traps them easily, lowering the spore load you breathe. What a purifier cannot do is kill or remove mold colonies on surfaces; the [EPA stresses](https://www.epa.gov/mold/brief-guide-mold-moisture-and-your-home) that controlling moisture and physically removing mold is the fix. See our [mold protection guide](/air-purifier/mold-protection) for how the stages work.

| Activated carbon | Adsorbs musty MVOC odors mold produces |

| Right-sized coverage | Enough air changes to keep spore counts low |

## Why does UV-C matter specifically for mold?

Standard purifiers capture spores but leave them alive on the filter, where humidity can let them germinate and shed again. The AirPro's enclosed UV-C neutralizes captured spores, keeping the filter a permanent trap. This is the key difference in damp spaces — more in [why UV-C matters for mold](/air-purifier/mold-protection).

## Where should I run an air purifier for mold?

Prioritize the dampest, most-used rooms: basements, bathrooms, bedrooms, and any space with past water damage. The AirPro clears about 430 sq ft in 10 minutes, so it reduces airborne spores quickly after showers, cooking, or humidity spikes. Pair it with a dehumidifier to control the moisture that feeds mold — see [air purifier vs. dehumidifier](/air-purifier/air-purifier-vs-dehumidifier) and [where to place an air purifier](/air-purifier/where-to-place-an-air-purifier).

## Does an air purifier replace mold remediation?

No. Think of it as harm reduction for the air while you address the root cause. Fix leaks and humidity, clean or professionally remediate visible mold, and run the AirPro to lower airborne exposure in the meantime. For the health context, see [mold protection](/air-purifier/mold-protection).
