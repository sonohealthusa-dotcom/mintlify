## Quick Questions

**What MERV rating is HEPA 14 equivalent to?**
HEPA 14 is beyond the MERV scale. MERV tops out at 16 (~95% at 0.3 microns), while HEPA 14 captures 99.995% at 0.3 microns under EN 1822 — which is why HEPA grades use the EN 1822 scale, not MERV.

**Does the AirPro capture ultrafine PM1 and diesel particles?**
Yes. Its HEPA 14 filter captures 99.9% of particles down to 0.1 microns, covering PM1 and most ultrafine traffic and combustion particles — finer than the PM2.5 most purifiers target.

**How much clearance does it need?**
A few inches of open space on all sides so the 360-degree intake can draw air freely — avoid corners and don't tuck it under furniture. See [Where to Place an Air Purifier](/air-purifier/where-to-place-an-air-purifier).

**Do I still need a purifier if I have central AC or a good furnace filter?**
Usually yes. An AC cools the air and a furnace filter only cleans while the HVAC fan runs and only to its MERV grade. A portable HEPA 14 purifier cleans the air in your occupied room continuously and to a far higher standard. See [Air Purifier vs. Air Conditioner](/air-purifier/air-purifier-vs-air-conditioner) and [Air Purifier vs. HVAC Furnace Filter](/air-purifier/air-purifier-vs-hvac-furnace-filter).

**Is the AirPro worth it over a cheap $50 air purifier?**
Budget units often use unrated "HEPA-type" filters, a weak fan sized for 100–200 sq ft, and sometimes an ozone-emitting ionizer. The AirPro uses verified HEPA 14, covers up to 1,200 sq ft, adds carbon and enclosed ozone-free UV-C, and includes a 60-day trial. See [AirPro vs. Cheap Air Purifiers](/air-purifier/airpro-vs-cheap-air-purifiers).
