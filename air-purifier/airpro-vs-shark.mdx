## Filtration: HEPA 14 vs. Shark's True HEPA

Shark's HEPA purifiers use a **True HEPA (H13-class)** filter, which captures roughly 99.97% of particles at 0.3 microns — genuinely good filtration. The AirPro is rated one grade higher to **HEPA 14 (99.995% at 0.3 microns)** under the EN 1822 standard, letting through about **10&times; fewer particles** at the hardest-to-catch size, and it adds a **UV-C** stage Shark models don't include. See [HEPA 14 vs. True HEPA](/air-purifier/hepa-14-vs-true-hepa) for what that gap means in practice.

## What about Shark's "NeverChange" filter?

Some Shark models advertise a **long-life or "NeverChange" filter** designed to last several years, which is convenient and lowers how often you handle a filter. The trade-off is that a very long-life filter still loads with particles over time, and you cannot swap in a fresh certified HEPA 14 element. The AirPro instead uses a **replaceable HEPA 14 filter** (6–12 months, tracked by the unit) so filtration stays at full grade. Filters are sold on the [filter page](https://sonohealth.com/products/airpro-replacement-filter-compatible-with-airpro-purifier), with a discount on the [filter subscription](/air-purifier/filter-replacement).

## Where Shark wins

Shark is a strong mass-market choice: wide retail availability, simple controls, and on NeverChange models, years between filter changes. If you want a straightforward purifier from a familiar brand and True HEPA is enough for your needs, Shark is a reasonable pick.
