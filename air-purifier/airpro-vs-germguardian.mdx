## Filtration grade: HEPA 14 vs. True HEPA

GermGuardian's HEPA towers use **True HEPA (H13-class)** filters marketed around 99.97% at 0.3 microns. The AirPro is rated to the higher **HEPA 14** grade — **99.995% at 0.3 microns under EN 1822** — capturing roughly 10x fewer particles at the hardest-to-trap size, and 99.9% down to 0.1 microns. If filtration grade is your priority, the AirPro sits a full class above. See [HEPA 14 vs. True HEPA](/air-purifier/hepa-14-vs-true-hepa).

## Room coverage: small room vs. whole living space

Most GermGuardian towers are sized for a **single small room** (roughly 150-190 sq ft by AHAM room-size ratings). The AirPro cleans **430 sq ft in about 10 minutes** and up to **1,200 sq ft in 30 minutes** with 360-degree intake, so one unit covers a living room, open-plan space, or large bedroom. Match coverage to your room in [Room Coverage](/air-purifier/room-coverage).

## UV-C and ozone

The AirPro includes a **fully enclosed UV-C (254 nm)** stage as standard, with **no ozone** because there's no ionizer. Some GermGuardian models add UV-C, and a few pair it with an ionizer or PCO stage — if you want to avoid any ozone-generating technology, check the specific model. The AirPro keeps it simple: mechanical HEPA 14 + carbon + enclosed UV-C, ozone-free. See [UV-C Sterilization](/air-purifier/uv-c-sterilization) and [HEPA vs. Ionizer](/air-purifier/hepa-vs-ionizer-air-purifiers).
