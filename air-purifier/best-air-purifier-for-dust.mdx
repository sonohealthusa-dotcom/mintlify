The SonoHealth AirPro combines a pre-filter, medical-grade HEPA 14 (99.9% of particles down to 0.1 microns), and 360° airflow across up to 1,200 sq ft. A purifier reduces airborne dust — it does not replace regular cleaning of settled dust.

## Can an air purifier actually reduce dust?

Yes — for the airborne fraction. Fine dust and dust-mite allergen particles float and recirculate, and a HEPA 14 filter captures them easily, lowering what you breathe and what resettles on surfaces. What a purifier cannot do is remove dust already lying on floors and shelves, so it works best alongside vacuuming with a HEPA vacuum and regular wiping. ## Does it help with dust-mite allergies?

It can reduce the airborne allergen load. Dust mites themselves live in bedding and upholstery, but their allergen-carrying particles become airborne when you move around; capturing those lowers exposure. Combine the AirPro with hot-washing bedding and reducing humidity, since mites thrive in damp air. For allergy context see [Best Air Purifier for Allergies](/air-purifier/best-air-purifier-for-allergies).
