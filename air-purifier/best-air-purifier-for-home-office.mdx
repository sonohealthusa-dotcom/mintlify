The best air purifier for a home office is quiet enough for video calls, ozone-free, and strong enough to clean the whole workspace of dust, pollen, and VOCs. Cleaner indoor air is associated with better comfort and focus, so medical-grade HEPA filtration plus an activated-carbon stage matters most. The SonoHealth AirPro HEPA 14 meets each of these.

## Why does air quality matter when you work from home?

Home offices collect dust, printer and electronics off-gassing, and outdoor pollen that drifts in — and you may spend eight or more hours a day in the room. The EPA notes indoor air can carry higher pollutant levels than outdoor air, and reducing fine particles and VOCs indoors can lower daily exposure. See [Indoor Air Quality Guide](/air-purifier/air-quality-guide).

<Note>
An air purifier is a wellness appliance, not a medical device. It reduces airborne particles and some gases but does not treat any condition or replace good ventilation.
</Note>

## What should you look for in a home-office air purifier?

- **Quiet on calls.** A loud unit is distracting on video. The AirPro runs **25 dB in Night Mode** (quieter than a whisper) and 25–50 dB overall, so it won't compete with your microphone.
- **Whole-room coverage.** The AirPro cleans **430 sq ft in about 10 minutes** and up to **1,200 sq ft in 30 minutes** with 360-degree intake — enough for a home office plus adjoining space. See [Room Coverage](/air-purifier/room-coverage).
- **VOC and odor control.** An **activated-carbon** stage handles printer fumes, new-furniture off-gassing, and cooking smells that drift in. See [VOC & Formaldehyde Guide](/air-purifier/voc-formaldehyde-guide).
- **Medical-grade HEPA.** HEPA 14 (99.995% at 0.3 microns, EN 1822) captures dust, pollen, and fine PM2.5 — see [HEPA 14 Filtration](/air-purifier/hepa-14-filtration).
- **Ozone-free.** The AirPro uses no ionizer and emits no ozone, so it's comfortable to run all day at your desk.

## Where should I put it in my office?

Give the unit a few inches of clearance on all sides so its 360-degree intake can draw freely — avoid tucking it under the desk or into a tight corner. A spot near where you sit, but not blowing directly on paperwork, works well. See [Where to Place an Air Purifier](/air-purifier/where-to-place-an-air-purifier).
