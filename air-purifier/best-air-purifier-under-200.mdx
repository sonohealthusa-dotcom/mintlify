## Why HEPA 14 in This Price Range Is Unusual

HEPA 14 filters cost more to make than HEPA 13 because they use denser fiber media and are tested to the EN 1822 standard. Many brands use H13-class filters marketed as "True HEPA." The AirPro uses a HEPA 14 filter.
