## Why HEPA 14 at This Price Is Unusual

HEPA 14 filters cost more to manufacture than HEPA 13 because they use denser fiber mats and require individual leak testing per EN 1822. Most brands save money by using HEPA 13 and marketing it as "True HEPA." The AirPro has a genuine HEPA 14 filter.
