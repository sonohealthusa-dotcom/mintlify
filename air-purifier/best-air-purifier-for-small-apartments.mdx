For a small apartment, studio, condo, or dorm, the best air purifier is one compact unit with more coverage than the space needs, quiet night operation, and zero ozone. The SonoHealth AirPro fits that profile: a single unit covers **up to 1,200 sq ft** — well beyond most apartments — runs as low as **25 dB**, draws air from all sides, needs no installation, and produces **no ozone**, which matters most in small, enclosed rooms.

## How much coverage do you actually need?

Most small apartments and studios fall between roughly 400 and 900 sq ft. Because the AirPro cleans **430 sq ft in about 10 minutes and up to 1,200 sq ft in 30 minutes**, a single unit comfortably covers a typical apartment, and in one open room it will cycle the air several times an hour. Match coverage to your floor plan using [Room Coverage](/air-purifier/room-coverage) rather than buying more purifier than you need.

<Note>
For a very small single room (under ~150 sq ft), the AirPro is more capacity than you strictly need — run it on a lower speed or Night Mode. Its value in small homes is covering the *whole* apartment from one spot.
</Note>

## Why noise matters more in a small space

In a studio or dorm, you sleep, work, and relax in the same room as the purifier, so a loud unit is a dealbreaker. The AirPro's **Night Mode runs at 25 dB** — quieter than a whisper — and dims its lights, so it can run beside your bed all night. On higher speeds it reaches about 50 dB for a fast clean when you're awake.

## Why ozone-free is non-negotiable in a small room

Some purifiers use ionizers that can emit **ozone**, a lung irritant — a bigger concern in a small, enclosed apartment where that air has nowhere to go. The AirPro uses **no ionizer and produces no ozone**; it cleans only by mechanical HEPA 14 filtration, activated carbon, and fully enclosed UV-C. See [HEPA vs. Ionizer Air Purifiers](/air-purifier/hepa-vs-ionizer-air-purifiers).

## Placement tips for apartments and dorms

Give the AirPro a **few inches of clearance on all sides** so its 360-degree intake can draw freely — avoid corners, tight nooks, and tucking it under a desk or bed. In a studio, a spot near where you spend the most time (bed or desk) works best. It sits on the floor or a sturdy shelf; see [Where to Place an Air Purifier](/air-purifier/where-to-place-an-air-purifier).

## Is it good for renters?

Yes. The AirPro is freestanding and needs no installation, ducting, or landlord approval, so it moves with you. One unit is easy to relocate between an apartment now and a larger place later, and each unit qualifies for the 60-day free trial and 2-year warranty (lifetime with a filter subscription).
