## Filtration grade: HEPA 14 vs. True HEPA

Alen's filters are marketed in the **True HEPA (H13) class**, with some options advertised around 99.99% at 0.1 microns. The AirPro's **HEPA 14** filter is certified to **99.995% at 0.3 microns** under EN 1822 — the standardized, most-penetrating-particle-size benchmark — and captures 99.9% down to 0.1 microns. When comparing, look for a stated **EN 1822 grade** rather than a marketing percentage at a hand-picked particle size. See [HEPA 14 vs. True HEPA](/air-purifier/hepa-14-vs-true-hepa).

## Smart sensor vs. built-in UV-C

This is the real trade-off. The **Alen leads on automation**: its SmartSensor reads air quality and adjusts fan speed automatically, which is genuinely convenient. The **AirPro leads on sterilization and simplicity**: a fully enclosed UV-C (254 nm) stage neutralizes microbes on the filter, and you control it with manual fan speeds, a 2/4/6-hour Smart Timer, and a 25 dB Night Mode — no app or subscription required. Learn more in [UV-C Sterilization](/air-purifier/uv-c-sterilization) and [Smart Features](/air-purifier/smart-features).
