Your liver, kidneys, gut, lungs, and skin already detoxify your body continuously — so most commercial "detoxes" and "cleanses" (juice fasts, colon cleanses, detox teas) aren't necessary and aren't proven to remove toxins. The words are often used interchangeably in marketing, but the honest takeaway is the same: **supporting your natural pathways with hydration, fiber, sleep, and reduced exposure is more useful than any harsh cleanse.**

<Note>
This page is educational and not medical advice. If you think you've had a genuine toxic exposure (heavy metals, mold, a poisoning), that's a medical situation — see a clinician; a supplement or cleanse is not the answer. These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA.
</Note>

## What people usually mean by each word

- **Detox** in medicine means treating a real, acute poisoning or withdrawal (for example, alcohol detox) under professional care. In wellness marketing, "detox" is used loosely for products claimed to remove everyday "toxins."
- **Cleanse** usually refers to a short-term regimen — a juice fast, a colon cleanse, or a tea program — meant to "reset" the body. There's no standard definition, and effects are mostly from calorie restriction and water loss, not toxin removal.

In everyday product language the two blur together. Neither, as sold, has strong human evidence for pulling toxins out of a healthy body.

## What the evidence actually says

Reviews of "detox diets" and cleanse programs have repeatedly found **little to no rigorous evidence** that they eliminate toxins or provide lasting benefit. Any short-term "lighter" feeling usually comes from eating fewer processed foods, cutting alcohol, and drinking more water — good habits you can keep without an expensive kit. Aggressive colon cleanses can even cause dehydration and electrolyte problems.

## Your body's real detox system

You already have a capable, always-on system:

- **Liver** — chemically transforms substances so they can be eliminated.
- **Kidneys** — filter the blood and remove waste in urine.
- **Gut** — moves waste out; fiber and hydration keep it regular.
- **Lungs and skin** — clear volatile compounds and support the process.

The most useful thing you can do is **support** these organs, not "override" them with a cleanse.

## How to actually support your natural pathways

- Eat plenty of fiber-rich whole foods (vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains).
- Stay well hydrated.
- Prioritize sleep, movement, and limiting alcohol.
