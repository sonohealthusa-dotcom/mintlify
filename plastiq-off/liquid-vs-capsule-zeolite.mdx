Neither liquid nor capsule zeolite is proven superior for your body — what matters far more is the **type of zeolite (clinoptilolite), a clearly labeled dose, and third-party testing**. Liquid drops make it easy to take a precise, adjustable serving in water; capsules are convenient and taste-neutral. Choose the format you'll actually take every day.

<Note>
This is an educational comparison, not medical advice. No zeolite product "removes" microplastics or heavy metals from your body; binders work in the gut to support your natural elimination. See a clinician for any heavy-metal or mold concern.
</Note>

## How do the formats actually differ?

| Format | How it's taken | Strengths | Trade-offs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Liquid drops** | Droppers into water or straight | Easy to measure and adjust; no pills to swallow; mixes into a daily glass of water | Mild mineral taste; must be dosed with the dropper |
| **Capsules** | Swallowed with water | Convenient, portable, taste-neutral, pre-measured | Fixed dose; some people dislike swallowing pills |
| **Loose powder** | Stirred into liquid | Flexible dosing; often lowest cost per gram | Messy; easy to mis-measure; grittier texture |

## Does liquid zeolite absorb better than capsules?

There is **no strong human evidence** that one format is absorbed or "works" better. That claim is common in marketing but not well supported. What genuinely affects quality is whether the zeolite is **micronized clinoptilolite** (finer particles, more binding surface), where it's sourced, and whether it's tested for heavy metals — not whether it arrives as a drop or a pill.

## What should I actually look for?

- **Clinoptilolite specifically** — the well-studied zeolite type, not an unnamed "zeolite blend."
- **A stated amount per serving** — so you know your dose (for example, 100 mg).
- **Transparent sourcing** — made in the USA is a plus for traceability.
- **Third-party testing** — natural zeolite deposits vary in purity, so testing for heavy metals matters.
- **Honest claims** — "supports your body's natural detoxification pathways," not "flushes toxins."
