## What to look for in any zeolite product

Whatever brand you compare, the same green flags apply:

1. **Micronized clinoptilolite** from a disclosed source, ideally **made in the USA**.
2. **Third-party testing**, especially for lead and other heavy metals — ask for a current certificate of analysis. See [is zeolite safe?](/plastiq-off/is-zeolite-safe)
3. **Structure/function language** ("supports natural detox pathways") rather than removal claims ("removes toxins/heavy metals"), which aren't established in humans. See [do detox binders actually work?](/plastiq-off/do-detox-binders-work)
4. **A format you'll stick with** — liquid drops are easy to take daily and to mix in water.

## Why the added B12 matters

Most zeolite drops are zeolite-only. Plastiq Off pairs the zeolite with **200 mcg of active methylcobalamin B12**, which supports normal energy metabolism, red blood cell formation, and nervous system function. ## What both products can and can't do

Clinoptilolite zeolite works as a **gut-level binder** — it acts in the digestive tract, not as a magnet that pulls plastics or metals from your blood or organs. Neither product is a treatment for microplastic exposure, mold illness, or heavy-metal toxicity. If you're worried about a specific exposure, see a clinician.
