## Is "nano" zeolite better than micronized?

Not in any proven way. Marketing for nano-zeolite sprays often claims the particles are absorbed into the bloodstream to "hunt" toxins system-wide — a claim that goes beyond current human evidence. A gut-level binder like micronized clinoptilolite works in the digestive tract, which is where the best-supported binder activity happens. Judge products by sourcing, testing, and honest claims rather than particle-size buzzwords. See [micronized vs. nano zeolite](/plastiq-off/micronized-vs-nano-zeolite).

## What both products can and can't do

Clinoptilolite zeolite acts as a **gut-level binder** — it works in the digestive tract, not as a magnet that pulls plastics or metals out of your blood or organs. Neither product is a treatment for microplastic exposure, mold illness, or heavy-metal toxicity. If you're worried about a specific exposure, see a clinician.
