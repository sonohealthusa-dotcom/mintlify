## Why a binder isn't something you "feel"

Clinoptilolite zeolite acts as a **gut-level binder** — it stays in the digestive tract and isn't meaningfully absorbed into your bloodstream. Supporting your body's natural elimination is a quiet, background process, so there's usually no dramatic "it kicked in" moment. Be skeptical of any product that promises fast, obvious "detox" effects; those claims go beyond the evidence. See [do detox binders actually work?](/plastiq-off/do-detox-binders-work)

## What you might notice — and what you won't

- **Energy (from B12):** [Plastiq Off](/plastiq-off/overview) adds 200 mcg of active methylcobalamin, which supports normal energy metabolism and nervous-system function. If you were running low on B12, everyday-energy support is the most likely thing you'd notice over a few weeks. See the [NIH B12 fact sheet](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB12-HealthProfessional/).
- **No dramatic "cleanse":** Plastiq Off is a gentle daily routine, not a purge — you shouldn't expect urgent bathroom trips or a "flush."
- **Feeling worse is not a required "detox reaction":** there's no evidence you must feel bad for a binder to "work." If you feel off, make sure you're well hydrated, and if it continues, stop and check with a clinician.

## How to give it a fair trial

- Take it **daily** — 2 mL (2 droppers), directly or mixed in water, with a full glass of water. See [how to take Plastiq Off](/plastiq-off/how-to-take-plastiq-off).

## So how will I know it's "working"?

Honestly, you won't have a way to measure toxin levels at home, and no supplement clears microplastics already in your blood — a binder only *supports* natural elimination in the gut. The realistic goal is a **consistent daily habit** alongside the bigger wins: reducing plastic exposure, eating fiber, and staying hydrated. If you're taking it mainly for energy and don't notice any change after a few weeks, talk with your clinician about checking your B12.
