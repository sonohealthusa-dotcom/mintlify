There's no single "best" microplastic detox supplement, and **no supplement removes microplastics from your body** — so the smart move is to choose an honest, well-tested product that *supports* your body's natural elimination, and to pair it with reducing exposure and eating fiber. Look for a clean, transparently sourced binder from a company that doesn't over-promise.

<Note>
Educational buyer's guide. Be wary of any product claiming to "flush" or "remove" microplastics or "detox" your body — those claims aren't supported and are the kind regulators act on. Honest structure/function claims are the green flag.
</Note>

## What to look for

- **A clearly labeled binder** — e.g., clinoptilolite zeolite, activated charcoal, or chlorella, at a stated amount.
- **Transparent sourcing** — where the zeolite is from (made in USA is a plus) and how it's processed (micronized = finer particles).
- **Third-party testing** — especially for heavy metals, since natural zeolite deposits vary in purity.
- **Honest claims** — "supports your body's natural detoxification pathways," not "removes all toxins."
- **Easy daily dosing** — something you'll actually take consistently (liquid drops vs. capsules).
- **A guarantee** — so you can try it risk-free.

## Red flags to avoid

- "Flushes/removes/eliminates microplastics or heavy metals"
- Claims to treat mold illness, disease, or "detox" as a cure
- No sourcing or testing information
- Mega-blends with dozens of ingredients at undisclosed amounts
