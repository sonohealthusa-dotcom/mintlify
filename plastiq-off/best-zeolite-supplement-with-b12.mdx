## Why pair zeolite with B12 at all?

Zeolite and B12 do different jobs, and combining them serves two everyday goals in one daily dose:

- **Zeolite (clinoptilolite)** acts as a gut-level binder to support your body's natural detoxification pathways.
- **Vitamin B12 (methylcobalamin)** supports normal energy metabolism, red blood cell formation, and nervous-system function.

Because many people exploring "detox" support are also interested in daily energy, a zeolite + active-B12 formula covers both without adding a second product.

## The checklist for a zeolite + B12 supplement

- **Clinoptilolite zeolite**, named specifically, with a **stated milligram amount** per serving.
- **B12 as methylcobalamin** (the active form) rather than only cyanocobalamin, at a meaningful amount.
- **Made in the USA / transparent sourcing** for the zeolite, since deposit purity varies.
- **Third-party tested**, especially for heavy metals.
- **A simple, honest label** — structure/function claims, not disease or "removal" claims.
- **A format you'll take daily** and a satisfaction guarantee so you can try it risk-free.
