Take **2 mL (2 droppers) of Plastiq Off once a day**, either straight or mixed into water, with a full glass of water — and space it **at least 2 hours from any medications**. That's the whole routine. Plastiq Off is a gentle daily supplement, not a harsh cleanse, so consistency matters more than dose size.

<Note>
These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA. Plastiq Off is a dietary supplement that supports the body's natural detoxification pathways and everyday energy; it is not a treatment for any disease. Ask your clinician before use if pregnant, nursing, on medication, or managing a health condition. Not for children.
</Note>

## The basic dose

| | |
|---|---|
| Serving | 2 mL — that's **2 full droppers** |
| How often | Once daily |
| How to take | Directly in the mouth, or stirred into water |
| With | A full glass of water |
| Servings per bottle | About **14** (roughly two weeks per bottle) |

There's no benefit to taking more than the labeled 2 mL — with binders, "more" doesn't mean "faster," and extra can just bind up nutrients. Stick to one serving a day.

## Timing: fit it around meals and medications

- **Space it 2 hours from medications and other supplements.** Binders can affect how other things are absorbed, so keep a gap. This is the single most important rule.
- **Food or empty stomach?** Either works. Many people take it first thing in the morning or between meals to keep the medication gap easy.
- **Because it contains active B12,** some people prefer the morning so the energy-metabolism support lands earlier in the day. It contains no caffeine or stimulants.

## Make it a habit that sticks

Because a bottle is about 14 servings, a once-daily routine runs through a bottle in roughly two weeks. To avoid gaps, many people keep a **two- or three-bottle supply** on hand. Anchor the dose to something you already do daily — your morning water, brushing your teeth — so you don't miss it. ## What to expect (and what not to)

Plastiq Off is designed for **gentle daily support**, so you shouldn't expect a dramatic "cleanse" feeling. It does **not** flush microplastics, heavy metals, or mold from your body — [no supplement does](/plastiq-off/do-detox-binders-work). What it offers is:

- **Detox-pathway support** from micronized clinoptilolite zeolite (a gut-level binder), and
- **Everyday energy support** from 200 mcg of active B12 (methylcobalamin).

<Warning>
If you feel worse, develop new symptoms, or suspect a real exposure (mold, heavy metals), stop and see your clinician. A "detox reaction" is not a required or expected part of taking a daily supplement.
</Warning>

## Staying comfortable

- **Hydrate.** The most common complaint with any binder is constipation from under-hydration — the full glass of water matters.
- **Sensitive stomach?** You can start with the dose in a larger glass of water and take it with a small meal.
- **Storage.** Keep it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. See [Product Facts](/plastiq-off/data) for full label details.

## Common questions

**Can I take it with my other SonoHealth supplements?** Yes — just keep the 2-hour gap from medications in mind. Spacing it from other mineral supplements (iron, zinc) is also sensible so the binder doesn't reduce their absorption.

**Do I need to cycle off?** Plastiq Off is formulated for daily use; there's no built-in requirement to cycle. If you prefer breaks or have a health condition, ask your clinician.

**How will I know it's working?** Detox-pathway support is subtle and not something you'll "feel" like a stimulant. Judge it as part of a broader routine of [reducing exposure](/plastiq-off/how-to-reduce-microplastics), fiber, and hydration — not as a quick fix.
