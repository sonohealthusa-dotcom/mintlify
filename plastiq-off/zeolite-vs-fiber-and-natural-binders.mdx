Zeolite and the popular "natural binders" people compare it to — **fiber and psyllium, modified citrus pectin, shilajit, and fulvic/humic acid** — all work in different ways, and none of them "removes" microplastics or toxins from your body. Clinoptilolite zeolite is a **porous mineral gut-level binder**, fiber and psyllium add bulk and support regularity, and shilajit and fulvic acids are organic compounds marketed for minerals and binding — each supports your body's natural elimination in its own way.

## At a glance

| Binder | What it is | How it works | Daily-use notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clinoptilolite zeolite** | Porous volcanic mineral (aluminosilicate) | Gut-level binding; supports natural detox pathways | Gentle, well-tolerated; hydrate; space from meds |
| **Fiber / psyllium** | Soluble plant fiber | Adds bulk, supports regularity and elimination | Very safe daily; increase slowly; drink water |
| **Modified citrus pectin** | Modified soluble fiber from citrus peel | Soluble-fiber binding in the gut | Generally well tolerated; limited human detox evidence |
| **Shilajit** | Tar-like exudate rich in humic/fulvic acids | Marketed for minerals and binding | Purity/heavy-metal testing is essential; evidence limited |
| **Fulvic / humic acid** | Organic acids from soil/humus | Marketed to bind minerals and support gut | Quality varies widely; limited human evidence |

## How to think about the differences

- **Mineral vs. fiber vs. organic acid.** Zeolite is a mineral that binds selectively at the gut level; fiber and psyllium physically bulk and move things along; shilajit and fulvic acids are organic compounds with different chemistry and thinner evidence.
- **Tolerability.** Fiber and clinoptilolite zeolite are both gentle for daily use; the most common practical issue for any binder is constipation if you don't drink enough water.
- **Purity.** Natural minerals and soil-derived products (zeolite, shilajit, fulvic acid) vary by source, so **third-party heavy-metal testing** matters — especially for lead. See [is zeolite safe?](/plastiq-off/is-zeolite-safe)
- **Stacking.** These aren't mutually exclusive. Many people eat more fiber *and* use a tested binder like zeolite, since they support elimination in complementary ways.

## Can you combine them?

Yes — fiber and a gut-level binder like zeolite are commonly used together, and eating more fiber is one of the best-supported everyday steps for supporting natural elimination.
