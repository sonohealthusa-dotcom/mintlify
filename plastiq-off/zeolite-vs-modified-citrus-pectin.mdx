Zeolite and modified citrus pectin (MCP) are two very different "binders": **clinoptilolite zeolite is a porous mineral that works in the gut, while MCP is a soluble fiber made from citrus peels that acts more like a gel-forming fiber**. Both are used to *support* the body's natural elimination pathways, and neither is proven to remove toxins already in your blood or tissues. Plastiq Off uses micronized zeolite plus active B12.

## How each one works

- **Clinoptilolite zeolite** is a naturally porous, negatively charged aluminosilicate mineral. In the digestive tract it behaves as a **gut-level binder**. It is not meaningfully absorbed into the body. See [is zeolite safe?](/plastiq-off/is-zeolite-safe)
- **Modified citrus pectin** is citrus-peel pectin processed into shorter chains so it dissolves more easily. It's essentially a **soluble fiber** that forms a gel in the gut and is studied mostly for that fiber-like activity.

## Which should I choose?

Neither is proven superior for "detox," so the practical questions are what fits your routine and tolerance. Zeolite in a liquid like [Plastiq Off](/plastiq-off/overview) is a low-volume 2 mL daily dose that also delivers active B12 for everyday energy. MCP is a fiber and is usually taken in larger gram doses; if your goal is simply more soluble fiber, food sources and a fiber supplement may do the same job. See [zeolite vs. fiber and other natural binders](/plastiq-off/zeolite-vs-fiber-and-natural-binders).

## Can I take both together?

It's best not to stack multiple binders at the same time — they all work in the gut and can bind nutrients and medications, raising the chance of interference or constipation. If you use more than one, space them out, stay well hydrated, keep them at least 2 hours from medications, and check with your clinician. See [how to take Plastiq Off](/plastiq-off/how-to-take-plastiq-off).

## What neither can do

Both work in the digestive tract, not as a magnet pulling microplastics or metals from your blood or organs. Neither is a treatment for microplastic exposure, mold illness, or heavy-metal toxicity.
