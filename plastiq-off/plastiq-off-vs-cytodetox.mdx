## Be cautious about "cellular" or "nano" zeolite claims

Some liquid zeolite products are marketed with language like "cellular," "nano-sized," or "cleansed" zeolite that is claimed to travel through your bloodstream and pull out toxins. Human evidence for that mechanism is limited, and clinoptilolite is best understood as a **gut-level binder** that acts in the digestive tract. Favor products that describe what they do in plain structure/function terms and can share a certificate of analysis (COA), especially for lead and aluminum.
