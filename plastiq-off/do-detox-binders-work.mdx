Detox binders like clinoptilolite zeolite, activated charcoal, and chlorella can bind certain substances **within the digestive tract**, but there is **no strong human evidence that any supplement "removes microplastics, heavy metals, or toxins" from your body as a whole**. The honest framing is that binders may *support* your body's natural elimination pathways — not that they flush toxins out.

<Note>
This page is educational and not medical advice. If you think you've been exposed to a specific toxin or have symptoms you're worried about, see a clinician — some exposures need real medical testing and treatment, not a supplement.
</Note>

## What "binding" actually means

Binders are porous or charged materials that substances can stick to. In the **gut**, that can mean a binder holds onto certain molecules that are then passed in stool. This is well established for specific clinical uses — for example, activated charcoal in some acute poisoning situations, given in a hospital. It is a *local, in-the-gut* effect, not a magnet that pulls plastics or metals out of your blood, brain, or organs.

## What the research supports — and what it doesn't

- **Supported:** Clinoptilolite zeolite is generally well tolerated in humans, and lab and animal work shows it can bind some substances in the gut.
- **Limited / preliminary:** Human evidence that zeolite or other binder supplements meaningfully lower body burdens of heavy metals or "detoxify" people is small, mixed, and preliminary.
- **Not supported:** Claims that a supplement "flushes," "removes," or "eliminates" microplastics or toxins from your body. Regulators have taken action against sellers making exactly these claims.

## How to read binder claims (green flags vs. red flags)

**Green flags**

- "Supports your body's natural detoxification pathways" (structure/function language)
- A named binder (e.g., clinoptilolite zeolite) at a **stated dose**
- Transparent sourcing and **third-party testing** for heavy metals
- Acknowledges that reducing exposure matters most

**Red flags**

- "Removes/flushes/eliminates microplastics, heavy metals, or toxins"
- "Treats mold illness," "chelates," "detoxes your organs," or any disease claim
- No dose, no sourcing, no testing
- Dozens of ingredients at undisclosed amounts
